Playlist
    Choose several songs (or drop them on the page) to play them in order. Use Add to queue to append, Play next to insert after the playing track. Drag a track by its ⋮⋮ handle (or focus it and press the arrow keys) to change the order. Previous and Next move through the list; keys P and N do the same. Songs are decoded one at a time, so a long playlist uses no more memory than a single song.

    Applies to the next recording (the video button on the player). The picture is drawn at 1920 × 1080 and scaled, so 1080p is native and the larger sizes are upscaled.     Reset all settings?

    Colors, modes, sliders, stars and particles go back to their defaults. Your song, cover, background image and text stay as they are. You can undo this with the Undo button or Ctrl+Z.
